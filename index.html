What we do

The whole logistics value chain, under one roof

From freight coordination and customs clearance to warehousing and final delivery — we keep cargo moving from origin to destination.

Freight Forwarding

We coordinate the movement of your cargo across every mode of transport, choosing the route and carrier that fit your budget and deadline.

  • Air, sea, road and rail transportation
  • Route optimisation and carrier coordination
  • Shipment tracking and global shipping support

Customs Brokerage (Clearing)

Customs is where shipments stall. We prepare, file and follow through on your entries so cargo clears without avoidable delay or penalty.

  • Import and export documentation
  • Duty and tax processing
  • Regulatory compliance with local and international laws

Documentation Services

Every shipment carries a paper trail. We prepare it correctly the first time and manage it from booking through to final delivery.

  • Invoices, Bills of Lading, Certificates of Origin, Packing Lists
  • End-to-end documentation management
  • Records kept consistent across carriers and authorities

Supply Chain Management

Beyond single shipments, we help you plan the flow of goods — how much to hold, where to hold it and how it reaches your customers.

  • Logistics planning and advisory
  • Inventory control and coordination
  • Warehousing and distribution solutions

Specialised Logistics

Cargo that needs more than a standard lane — bonded storage, third-country trades and deliveries to the final address.

  • Bonded warehousing
  • Cross-trade operations
  • Last-mile delivery solutions

Transportation Services

Our own transport arrangements move goods across Liberia and beyond, shaped around what you are carrying and when it is needed.

  • Reliable and flexible cargo delivery
  • Customised transport solutions based on client needs
  • Coordination with your own schedules and sites
